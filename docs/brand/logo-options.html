Mabel Home Help: logo options

Six directions for the brand mark, each shown large, with the name on a light and a dark background, at small sizes, and in one colour (for stamps, invoices and embroidery).

OPTION 1

Roof and M

RECOMMENDED

An M for Mabel standing under a roof, with a heart where the two meet. It is the option that most belongs to this name alone, it stays clear when very small, and it is the one on the website now.
